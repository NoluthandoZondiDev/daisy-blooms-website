## Project Overview
Daisy Blooms Salon is a hypothetical family-run neighbourhood hair and
beauty salon offering haircuts, styling, colouring, and beauty
treatments. The salon currently has no website and relies on phone
calls and WhatsApp messages for bookings, which has led to repeated
double-bookings. This project builds a five-page website to give the
salon a professional online presence and a structured way for clients
to make enquiries.

This repository documents Part 1 (Building the Foundation) of a
three-part project. Part 2 (CSS styling and responsive design) and
Part 3 (JavaScript functionality and SEO) will follow in later
submissions.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- Reduce double-bookings by moving enquiries away from informal
  phone/WhatsApp scheduling.
- Showcase services and indicative pricing to attract new local
  clients.
- Build a professional online presence reflecting the salon's brand.
- Provide an easy way for clients to make service enquiries.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** number of monthly
enquiry-form submissions; reduction in scheduling conflicts reported
by staff; number of monthly website visits; time spent on the
Services page.

## Key Features and Functionality
- Home page with hero introduction, services preview, and gallery
  preview.
- About page with salon history, mission/vision, and team profiles.
- Services page with a full breakdown of services and indicative
  pricing.
- Enquiry page with a service enquiry form.
- Contact page with two branch locations, embedded maps, opening
  hours, and a general contact form.
- Consistent navigation menu across all five pages.
- Planned (Part 2/3): rose-gold glow hover effects on cards, buttons,
  and gallery images; responsive design; JavaScript form validation.
