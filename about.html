Our Story

Daisy Blooms Salon started as a single chair in a Rosebank spare room, run by Daisy Nkosi after years of styling family and friends on weekends. Word of mouth grew quickly, and within three years the salon moved into its own space, adding a second stylist and a colour specialist to keep up with demand. Today, the salon serves two Johannesburg neighbourhoods and is still run with the same personal touch it started with.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make every client feel confident and cared for, one appointment at a time. We believe great hair and beauty care shouldn't feel rushed or impersonal, so we keep our client list small enough to know people by name and by the styles they love.
